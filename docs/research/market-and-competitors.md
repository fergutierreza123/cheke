# cheke ventas: market, competitors and viability (Honduras + Central America)

Research date: **2026-10-16**. All web sources were accessed on 2026-10-16 unless stated. Exchange rate used throughout: **L26.90 per US$1** (BCH reference rate, 6 Oct 2026: L26.8920 buy / L27.0265 sell, reported by [La Prensa](https://www.laprensa.hn/economia/honduras-precio-dolar-martes-6-octubre-tipo-cambio-bch-JG32255268)). cheKe prices below are **before ISV (15%)**: L790 ≈ US$29.37, L1,590 ≈ US$59.11, L3,990 ≈ US$148.33.

Confidence tags: **[primary]** = the owner of the number (statistics office, Meta, the vendor's own pricing page). **[secondary]** = press or a third party quoting someone. **[low]** = unverified, old, or self-reported marketing claim. **[assumption]** = my modelling input, not a fact.

## Executive summary

1. **Nouvora could not be found.** No website, social profile, registry or press mention of a Honduran company called "Nouvora" turned up. nouvora.com, .ai and .co are parked domains, and @nouvora on Instagram is a Brazilian online shop. The closest Honduran matches are **NOUVA** (a Tegucigalpa consultancy that includes "AI automation") and **Wapster** (a Tegucigalpa omnichannel WhatsApp platform run by Chattigo). The founder should share Nouvora's link so it can be profiled (§1).
2. **The incumbent is Meta.** **Meta Business Agent** launched globally on 3 Jun 2026: an AI that answers, recommends from the catalog and "closes sales" on WhatsApp and Messenger, with Instagram coming ([Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/06/meta-business-agent/)). Since 15 Sep 2026 it is bundled in **Meta One Essential from US$14.99/mo** ([Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-meta-one-subscription-service-more-features-ai/)). That is half the price of cheKe's entry plan, and cheKe's entry plan has no chekelin.
3. **Honduras has 288,724 registered establishments** (INE DEE 2024 [primary]): 74.8% micro, 10.7% small, 1.8% medium. **57% are in commerce** and **13% in food/lodging**. **Distrito Central has 70,621 (24.5%)** and San Pedro Sula 19,533 ([INE](https://ine.gob.hn/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Publicacion-del-Directorio-DEE-Honduras-2024.pdf)). Business groups claim about 1.2M businesses in total, mostly informal [secondary/low].
4. **Digital reach is good on Meta, weak on payments.** Internet reaches 72.4% of the population, Facebook ad reach 4.6M (41.6%), Instagram 2.2M (19.9%) and Messenger 2.4M (DataReportal 2026 [primary-aggregator]). But only **2.5% of Honduran internet users went online to shop** (INE EPHPM Jul 2025 [primary]), and only about **42% of adults have an account** (Findex 2025 via E&N [secondary]).
5. **Labour informality is 74.8%** (INE, Nov 2024 [primary]). The target buyer is a semi-formal shop that sells through Instagram and WhatsApp. Many cannot issue or receive a factura, and many will not pay by card.
6. **Global tools exist but fit badly.** Kommo starts at US$25/user with a 6-month minimum and no monthly billing. Leadsales costs US$97–133, plus US$87+ for its AI agent. respond.io costs US$99, Wati about US$69, Botmaker US$149 and Cliengo US$45–119. None offers lempira billing, a factura, in-person onboarding or Honduran payment-link habits. **Avify** is the closest analog: a WhatsApp + IG + Messenger CRM with an AI agent, payment links and catalog, serving 7 countries including Honduras, with prices in USD and no public price list.
7. **TAM / SAM / SOM, base case:** TAM ≈ **L4.2B ≈ US$157M/yr**, SAM ≈ **L278M ≈ US$10.3M/yr** (about 18,850 businesses), SOM at end of Year 3 ≈ **400 paying tenants ≈ L5.9M ≈ US$220k ARR**. Conservative Y3 is about US$51k ARR and optimistic about US$656k (§5).
8. **Unit economics are healthy:** gross margin is about 75–98% per plan and blended contribution about US$40 per tenant per month. Break-even is **2 tenants on infra alone**, about **43 tenants** with a modest marketing team, and about **93** if the founder also draws US$2k/mo [assumption] (§8).
9. **Second market: El Salvador** (dollarized, so no FX or lempira pricing work; 76.8% internet; FB 63.6%, IG 32.2%; about 318k MYPEs in 2017; one main metro). Guatemala is bigger but less connected. Costa Rica is Avify's home turf (§6).
10. **Verdict:** this is **viable as a small, profitable Honduran business, not as a venture-scale one.** It wins only if cheKe sells *what Meta and Kommo don't*: a shared team inbox across all three channels, Honduran payment-link and transfer flows, a Honduran-Spanish agent, and in-person, done-for-you setup through the founder's real-estate tenants. It should also put some AI in the entry plan to answer Meta One's US$14.99 offer (§8).

## 1. Nouvora (Honduras)

**What was searched.** I ran web searches for "Nouvora" alone and with Honduras, Tegucigalpa, IA, chatbot, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn. I also probed nouvora.com, .ai, .co, .io, .com.hn, .hn, .tech, .app, .la and .net, plus Instagram and Facebook handles.

**Result: no Honduran company called Nouvora is publicly findable as of 2026-10-16.**
- nouvora.com, nouvora.ai and nouvora.co all return a JavaScript redirect to `/lander` (a parked or for-sale domain). The other TLDs do not resolve. [primary: direct HTTP check]
- instagram.com/nouvora is "Nouvora | Loja Online", a Portuguese-language shop with 723 followers and 0 posts. facebook.com/nouvora is the same "Loja Online" with 173 followers. Neither is Honduran.
- Exact-match web hits are a UK company, NOUVORA CLINICAL LIMITED ([Companies House](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/17335802/officers)), and a US cosmetics trademark ([Trademarkia](https://www.trademarkia.com/category/houseware-and-glass-products/page-n)). Neither is relevant.

**Closest Honduran matches** (in case the name was misheard):
- **NOUVA** (nouvacompany.com), Tegucigalpa. It describes itself as a consultancy "integrating specialized consultancy across finance, operations, sales, and AI automation". It also runs IP development, events and a podcast. Testimonials come from LABHOSPY, Butic The New School, Leasing Atlántida and Inversiones Petra. It cites "+10" collaborators, publishes no pricing, and **offers no self-serve omnichannel product** ([nouvacompany.com](https://www.nouvacompany.com); Facebook [nouva.company](https://www.facebook.com/nouva.company/)) [primary].
- **Wapster** (wapster.net), headquartered in Tegucigalpa and "operated by Chattigo". It combines the official WhatsApp Business API, AI chatbots, a multi-agent inbox, and Messenger, Instagram and webchat. It claims "más de 700 empresas clientes en 20 países". It has **no self-serve trial and no public pricing** (demo or info@wapster.net) ([wapster.net/llms.txt](https://wapster.net/llms.txt)) [primary, self-reported].

**How cheKe compares with this kind of player.** If Nouvora is like NOUVA or Wapster, it is a **case-by-case configurator** selling to mid-size and corporate accounts through demos and custom setup. cheKe is a **self-serve product priced for shops**: lempira plans, a phone-first UI and a 14-day trial. Both chase the same budget line ("atención y ventas por WhatsApp"). The overlap is in medium businesses and chains (cheKe Pro). They are unlikely to compete for single-phone shops, where a setup fee kills the deal.

**Action:** ask the founder for Nouvora's URL or Instagram handle. Without one, nothing more about them can be stated honestly.

**Is Business Agent available in Honduras?** Meta calls it global and in "local languages" but publishes no country list. **WhatsApp pricing changes** (all from cheKe's own research, issue #6, built on Meta's docs and rate cards):
- Per-message billing has applied since 1 Jul 2025 ([Meta](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing)).
- From **1 Oct 2026, service messages are billed.** Each number gets 1,000 free per month, then about **US$0.0113** each in "Rest of Latin America", which includes Honduras. Marketing costs US$0.074. Click-to-WhatsApp ad windows stay free.
- A shop with about 40 conversations a day pays about **US$46/mo** to Meta. One with about 8 a day pays about $0 ([issue #6 findings](https://github.com/fergutierreza123/cheke/blob/research/whatsapp-oct-2026-pricing/docs/research/whatsapp-oct-2026-pricing.md); also [Zoho](https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/community/topic/whatsapp-service-messages-pricing-update), [Gupshup](https://www.gupshup.ai/pricing)).

**AI policy on WhatsApp:**
- Since 15 Jan 2026, the WhatsApp Business Solution Terms bar "AI Providers" whose general-purpose assistant is the *primary* product. Business-specific customer service and sales bots are allowed, but Meta decides "in its sole discretion" ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/18/whatssapp-changes-its-terms-to-bar-general-purpose-chatbots-from-its-platform/)) [secondary]. chekelin is business-specific, so it is allowed today.
- Separately, WhatsApp's "restricted chat" feature helps users block "unofficial clients or AI agents" ([WhatsApp FAQ](https://faq.whatsapp.com/715385484388016/)) [primary].
- The Business Messaging Policy (updated 23 Sep 2026) requires a "vía clara y directa para llegar a una persona" ([Cliengo summary](https://guiawabusiness.cliengo.com/meta-business-agent)) [secondary]. chekelin's handoff already does this.

**What would make a shop pay for cheKe instead of Meta?**
1. **A team on one inbox across WhatsApp + IG + Messenger** with assignment, routing, an "Ausente" switch and per-agent analytics. The WhatsApp app tops out at 4 linked devices with no routing.
2. **A sales pipeline (Chekeo) and Confirmar pago leading to Ganado**, which Meta lacks.
3. **Honduran payment flows:** transfer details and links (PixelPay or Tilopay later). Meta's checkout is not live in Honduras.
4. **Comments to DM and complaint alerts** for Gerente and Dueño.
5. **In-person setup**, factura (SAR) and lempira billing.
6. **Cost per AI reply:** the repo estimates about US$10 for about 3,000 chekelin replies (about $0.003 each). Meta's API agent costs about $0.04–0.05 per message. Meta One's per-tier reply caps are unpublished.

If a shop is **one person on one WhatsApp number**, Meta (free, or $14.99 with AI) is "good enough". **cheKe's market is shops with 2+ people answering, or 2+ channels.**

## 7. Target market recommendation

**Best initial segments** (ranked) [assumption, informed by the DEE sector mix and 2026 reporting]:
1. **Fashion, boutiques, cosmetics, perfume and Shein-style importers** in Tegucigalpa. This is commerce, the 57% sector. They sell almost entirely via IG + WhatsApp ([La Prensa 2026](https://www.laprensa.hn/economia/auge-redes-sociales-impulsa-trabajo-cuenta-propia-honduras-GE29695751)), with high chat volume and repetitive questions ("¿precio?", "¿talla?", "¿envío?"). That is chekelin's sweet spot.
2. **Electronics, phone accessories and small appliances.** Many SKUs and price questions, often 2–4 staff on chat.
3. **Tenants of the founder's commercial properties:** physical stores that also sell on IG/WA. They are reachable in person, trust is pre-built, and they fit the pilot plan (issue #1).
4. **Restaurants and food delivery** (I sector, 13%). There is volume, but orders compete with delivery apps and need speed. Pursue after the order flow is proven.
5. **Real estate agents and auto dealers/parts.** These are lead-qualification flows, not catalog checkout. Good for cheKe Pro later, and the founder's network helps with real estate.

**Size:** micro and small businesses with **2–10 people**, of which 2+ answer chats. That is about the top quarter of micro plus most small businesses. Skip one-person sellers, who should use the free WhatsApp Business app or Meta One.

**City:** Distrito Central first (70,621 establishments; the founder is there). Then San Pedro Sula, the second commercial hub, which also has PixelPay.

**Buyer persona** (hypothesis to validate in pilots):
- *"Dueña de tienda"*, 25–40, runs a boutique, cosmetics or accessories shop in Tegucigalpa with an IG account (2k–30k followers) and a WhatsApp Business number shared on 2–3 phones.
- 20–100 chats a day, more at quincena and in December.
- Takes payment by bank transfer (screenshot), plus cash on delivery.
- Pain: lost chats and slow replies at night; staff answering from her phone.
- Buys when shown "ventas que se te están yendo".

**Acquisition channels that should work in Honduras:**
- In-person demos and setup at the founder's plazas (highest trust).
- **Click-to-WhatsApp ads on IG/FB.** These keep a 72-hour free window on Meta fees ([Meta pricing](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing)), and cheKe can sell itself through chekelin.
- Referral credit (one month free per referral).
- Local IG micro-influencers in "emprendimiento".
- Partnerships with Senprende and the Plan Nacional de IA y Comercio Electrónico training, CCIT/CCIC SMB programs, bank SME units (BAC, Ficohsa, Atlántida) and accountants who issue facturas.
- December is peak season for retail sales, so the launch timing helps.

## 8. Viability and impact

### 8a. Unit economics (US$/month, pre-ISV)

| | cheKe (L790) | Plus (L1,590) | Pro (L3,990) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | $29.37 | $59.11 | $148.33 |
| chekelin (Sonnet 5.5) | $0 (no chekelin) | ~$10 heavy (repo estimate, 3,000 replies) | ~$33 if 10,000 replies (scaled from the repo figure) [assumption] |
| AI photos | ~$0.50 [assumption] | ~$1.50 [assumption] | ~$4 [assumption] |
| **Contribution** | **~$28.9 (98%)** | **~$47.6 (81%)** | **~$111 (75%)** |
| WhatsApp fees | paid by the tenant on its own WABA (issue #6) | same | same |

- **Blended contribution** (base mix 60/35/5) ≈ **US$40 per tenant per month**.
- **Fixed infrastructure** ≈ US$68/mo (repo).
- Billing is by bank transfer + factura, so no card fees.

**Break-even tenants** = Fixed costs / blended contribution:
- Infra only: $68 / $40 → **2 tenants**.
- Plus marketing team and tools (≈ $1,500 + $150) [assumption] → $1,718 / $40 → **~43 tenants**.
- Plus a founder draw of $2,000 [assumption] → $3,718 / $40 → **~93 tenants**.
- The pilot discount (50% for 3 months) roughly halves contribution during ramp-up. Plan for a 3–4 month lag.

At base-case SOM (400 tenants by end Y3), the business clears about **US$16k/month contribution** before fixed costs. That is a healthy small business, not a venture-scale outcome on Honduras alone.

### 8b. Main risks

1. **Meta platform dependency.**
   - Meta can change pricing (service messages now billed), app review rules, Coexistence behaviour or the AI Provider terms, where Meta decides "in its sole discretion".
   - WhatsApp account bans already hit Honduran SMBs ([La Prensa, Apr 2026](https://www.laprensa.hn/honduras/mipymes-honduras-bloqueo-cuentas-whatsapp-business-HD30226753)). A tenant's ban becomes cheKe's churn.
   - Mitigation: stay squarely "business-specific customer service and sales", keep human handoff obvious, and never support bulk unsolicited messaging.
2. **The free or cheap incumbent.** Meta Business Agent + Meta One Essential at **$14.99** sets the price anchor for "AI on WhatsApp". If Meta adds IG + team inbox + checkout in Honduras, cheKe's entry plan gets squeezed. Mitigation: compete on team, omnichannel, pipeline and payments, and consider a small chekelin quota (e.g., 300 replies) in the L790 plan.
3. **Low willingness to pay and total cost.**
   - Many shops can't justify L908/mo incl. ISV, let alone L1,828 + about US$46 in Meta fees.
   - Only 42% of adults are banked, and 2.5% of internet users shop online.
   - Mitigation: prove ROI in the 14-day trial ("chekelin cerró X ventas").
4. **The informal economy (74.8%).**
   - Informal shops may refuse ISV or factura, or lack a card for Meta's Billing Hub. Without a card, replies stop after 1,000 free (issue #6).
   - Mitigation: onboarding help to add a card; the price shown with ISV included.
5. **Churn.** SMB SaaS churn is typically several percent a month [assumption; no Honduran benchmark found]. Seasonal sellers will pause after December.
6. **Execution capacity.** A part-time solo founder carries support, onboarding and sales. Support load scales with tenants.
7. **Better-funded LatAm entrants:** Avify already lists Honduras, and Leadsales and Kommo have partner networks.

### 8c. Moats (realistic strength)

- **Local language and culture.** chekelin speaks Honduran Spanish (voseo, "¿a cómo?", lempiras) and knows local delivery and payment norms. *Medium:* easy to copy, but global tools rarely bother.
- **Payment links and transfer flow** (Confirmar pago leading to Ganado; PixelPay later). *Medium:* a workflow fit, not a tech moat.
- **In-person onboarding plus factura and lempira billing.** *Medium-high* for this segment. No global tool offers it.
- **The commercial real-estate network.** *High for the first 50–100 tenants:* a warm, trusted, physical channel competitors don't have. It doesn't scale beyond the founder's properties.
- **Switching costs once Chekeo history, catalog and team are in cheKe.** *Medium.*

### 8d. Honest verdict

- **Viable, with a narrow lane.** Honduras has enough urban, social-selling businesses with 2+ people on chat (about 6k–19k in the conservative and base SAM) to support **a few hundred paying tenants**. At cheKe's margins, that is **US$50k–220k ARR by Year 3 (conservative to base)**: profitable for a lean operation and meaningful income for a founder, but **not a venture-scale market on Honduras alone**. Central America (El Salvador next) is needed for more.
- **The biggest strategic fact of 2026 is Meta selling its own sales agent for US$14.99.** cheKe should not position itself as "an AI that answers WhatsApp". That is now a Meta commodity. Its pitch should be **"tu equipo y tus tres canales en un solo lugar, con un vendedor que cobra en lempiras"**: team inbox, Chekeo, payments, comments and in-person setup.
- **Recommended adjustments:**
  - Add a small chekelin quota to the L790 plan, or a L990 middle plan.
  - Show prices with ISV included.
  - Make the Meta-fee estimate visible at signup.
  - Track "ventas cerradas por chekelin" as the ROI headline.
  - Validate willingness to pay with the 3–5 pilots before December. The key test is whether pilots *keep paying* after the 50%-off period.
- **Kill or pivot signal:** fewer than 30 paying tenants by end of Y1 despite the real-estate channel, or Meta enabling team inbox + IG + checkout in Business Agent in Honduras.
